The idea

What's better than waiting for this week's Crumbl drop?

Getting the chance to beat them at their own game.

Introducing the Crumbl x Trader Joe's Bake Off — a first of its kind collaboration between America's most craveable cookie brand and everyone's favorite neighborhood staple. This isn't just a partnership. It's a cultural moment.

Fueled by fan participation and pure competitive energy, Crumbl and Trader Joe's are handing the recipe — literally — back to the people. Armed with the same Trader Joe's ingredients, fans and newcomers alike will go head-to-head, flooding feeds with UGC-driven content as they craft, bake, and battle it out for cookie supremacy.

Bridging the in-store experience with an unstoppable online conversation, this collaboration proves that the best community moments don't just live on shelves. They live in your For You Page.

Campaign Phases

1.

Crumbl's weekly reel launches with the slogan and #CrumblxTJBakeOff, inviting fans to recreate and compete.

2.

Fans and creators take over: baking, taste testing, and flooding feeds with their results.

3.

Trader Joe's drops an exclusive limited edition collab tote, as a physical memento of the internet's most delicious rivalry.

Why this works?

For Crumbl:

Expands their drop culture beyond the box and gives their fans a new way to obsess over the brand, cementing their status as the internet's favorite cookie.

For Trader Joe's:

Puts their products at the center of culture, introducing their brand to a new, passionate audience and giving loyal shoppers a whole new reason to show up.

For Fans:

They're not just spectators, they are the campaign. Their creativity becomes the story that turns an everyday grocery run and weekly drop into a cultural moment.
