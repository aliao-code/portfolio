Dippin' Dots

New Product Launch Dippin' Dots Mock Campaign Pitch

PROBLEM(S)

Many people see Dippin’ Dots as a once in a while treat (at theme parks, ice cream trucks), not an everyday ice cream choice. That makes repeat consumption and loyalty harder. Compared to brands in supermarkets/freezers everywhere, Dippin’ Dots isn’t top-of-mind at the moment of craving. The old slogan of “Ice Cream of the Future” doesn’t resonate with consumers anymore.
