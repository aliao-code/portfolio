Level Shoes

Level Shoes breaks into the US Market Digital Campaign

CHALLENGE

Level Shoes is Dubai’s luxury destination for everything: shoes, bags, and accessories. But as it looked to break into the U.S. market, the brand faced a new set of hurdles: low awareness, a different luxury landscape, and fierce competition from long-established retailers. Our objectives, as an acting creative agency partner, was to craft two campaigns that would one, increase Level Shoe’s brand awareness and two, increase trial and interest.

SOLUTION

To help Level Shoes stand out among the competitive US market, we positioned the brand as the ultimate destination for self-expression. A place where every shopper can discover their style and feel confident in what they wear. Our idea centered on one motto: fashion lovers may all look different, but they’re united by a shared passion for creativity, confidence, and personal style.

OWN YOUR LEVEL CAMPAIGN

Fashion is personal, and Level Shoes meets every shopper at their level of expression. We brought this to life through a cast of diverse micro-influencers, each with a distinctive fashion voice, showing that Level Shoes isn’t just a store, but a community-driven destination. Their diverse style perspectives showed that Level Shoes isn’t just curating products, it’s curating possibilities, each one ready to resonate with someone’s personal identity.

To bring the idea to life, we partnered with a curated group of Lehigh University creators who acted as U.S. based micro-influencers for this campaign. Each embodying a distinct fashion subculture (Y2K, luxury streetwear, athletic style), their perspectives revealed how flexible Level Shoes’ products translate across style types, affirming the brand’s relevance to a wide spectrum of fashion consumers.

To promote social awareness, each influencer would share content across their own channels exploring the theme “What confidence looks like to me.” Through styling videos and personal storytelling, they would spotlight the Level Shoes pieces that best expressed their identity. Followers were invited to join the conversation using, sharing the products that resonated with them and how style fuels their confidence.

TRY IT. STYLE IT. MAKE IT YOURS.

To drive trial and interest, we needed to shift passive awareness into active product exploration. To promote the campaign’s core message of self expression, we had our second campaign focused on the idea that a single item can be interpreted differently by every individual. To do this, we introduced #HowILevelStyle. This invited U.S. consumers to engage directly with Level Shoes’ assortment through Augmented Reality (AR) and community-led styling moments. By reframing trial as creative play, the challenge encouraged users to mix, match, and experiment with products in ways that felt personal and expressive. Partnering with a curated group of micro-influencers, we introduced the experience by testing items through AR and prompting their followers to join in.

The AR component lived across Instagram and TikTok, but this wasn’t the usual try-on filter. Users selected their favourite pieces from a curated lineup of shoes, clothing, and accessories, then snapped a photo of themselves. Instantly, the platforms generated a transformed image, teleporting them into a universe that matched the outfit’s vibe: a surreal, editorial-style world built for sharing. Instead of simply previewing shoes, bags, or accessories, users could see the possibilities and places their look could take them, imagining entire moments around the outfits they put together. Weekly filter drops kept the momentum high, introducing new worlds and new items to explore as the collection evolved.

Instagram / TikTok Filters

After interacting with the AR tools, users were guided to discover their luxury “Style Archetype” (e.g., The Minimalist Muse, The Midnight Maximalist, The Global Nomad, The Modern Icon). Each archetype unlocked a curated product edit on the Level Shoes website, allowing users to browse pieces aligned with their aesthetic and streamline their path to finding styles they loved.
