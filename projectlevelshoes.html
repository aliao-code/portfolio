About the project

Level Shoes breaks into the US Market Digital Campaign

Client Level Shoes
Services Marketing Research and Advertising
Tools Capcut
Year 2025

COMPANY OVERVIEW

In Spring 2025, Level Shoes partnered with Lehigh University's advertising class to develop a digital campaign supporting their e-commerce launch into the United States. Level Shoes is Dubai's most iconic luxury retail destination, with a 96,000 square foot flagship in Dubai Mall that has redefined what a shopping experience can be. With over 250 brands spanning top luxury houses to emerging designers, their offering includes names like Balenciaga, CELINE, Nike, and Santoni, alongside exclusive limited drops and shipping as fast as two days. Their brand identity is driven by an editorial aesthetic: produced entirely in-house to ensure every visual touchpoint reflects the same luxury standard as the brands they carry. Now, they're bringing that same world-class experience to the American market.

CHALLENGE

As Level Shoes looked to break into the U.S. market, the brand faced a new set of hurdles: low awareness, a different luxury landscape, and fierce competition from long-established American retailers. Our objective was to craft a digital campaign that would increase Level Shoe’s brand awareness.

SOLUTION

To help Level Shoes stand out among the competitive US market, we positioned the brand as the ultimate destination for self-expression. A place where every shopper can discover their style and feel confident in what they wear. So, we had our ad campaign idea center around one fashion lovers may all look different, but they’re united by a shared passion for creativity, confidence, and personal style.

LEVEL SHOES WILL ALWAYS MEET YOU AT YOUR LEVEL CAMPAIGN

We brought this to life through a cast of diverse fashion obssessed micro-influencers, each with a distinctive fashion voice, proving that Level Shoes is more than a store, it's a community-driven destination where everyone can find a piece of their identity.

Level Shoes Ad

To bring the video ad idea to life, we partnered with a curated group of Lehigh University creators who acted as U.S. based micro-influencers for this campaign. Each embodying a distinct fashion subculture (artist, dancer, athlete), their perspectives revealed how flexibly Level Shoes’ products translate across different style types, affirming the brand’s relevance to a wide spectrum of fashion consumers.

On the influencer's side...

To promote social awareness and drive awareness for Level Shoes, each influencer would share content across their own channels exploring the theme "What confidence looks like to me."

Through styling videos and personal storytelling, they would showcase pieces from Level Shoes that reflect their identity and the meaning behind every look. With personalized discount codes, they would encourage their followers to explore the collection and find pieces that resonate with their own style.

Followers were then invited into the conversation, sharing what fashion means to them and how style fuels the confidence that makes them, them.
